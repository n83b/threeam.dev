Payment Policy

Information about invoices, project payments and late-payment terms.

Three AM Web & IT will provide clients with itemised electronic and/or paper invoices that outline the request, terms, methods of payment and other details.

Invoice terms

Invoices carry a 14 day term of payment from the date of invoice unless expressed otherwise in writing. Payment can be made via direct bank transfer (preferred), credit card (via eWay) or cheque.

Project payment schedule

  • Commencement deposit: A deposit calculated at 30% of the total quoted project value plus GST is required prior to commencing work.
  • Progress payments: May be requested at stages of completion or milestones, depending on scope, size and nature of the project.
  • Closing payment: An invoice for the final sum is sent once quoted work has been completed.
  • Variations: Variations are invoiced as progress payments and itemised to reflect the specific variation.

Refer to the current policy in the live system before publishing, as payment methods and fees may change.
